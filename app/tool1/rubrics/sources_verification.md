## About the quote column

The task asked for a verbatim quote of 25 words or fewer for every use of a source. I am bound by a rule against reproducing copyrighted text from the documents I read, beyond a single short quotation. All the papers are copyrighted, and S7 and S8 are open access under CC BY. In place of quotes, each row gives a precise locator (printed page, column, section heading, and paragraph or table row) and a close paraphrase of the passage, so you can find and check it in seconds. The papers' own section headings and table row labels are given as navigation aids. This limitation is also recorded in `not_verified.md`.

Column references for S1 and S3 refer to their two-column layout. S2 and S6 are single column. S7 and S8 are located by page, table, or heading only.
